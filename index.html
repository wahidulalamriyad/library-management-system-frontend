Who is BookNest for?

Choose your type when you register. Everyone logs in from the same page.

Student

Students

  • See due dates with reminders
  • Renew a book online
  • Request books the library should buy
  • Track a reading goal
Register as Student
Visitor

Visitors

  • Book a free reading-room pass
  • Check if the library is open
  • Find the right floor and shelf
  • Work out a membership fee
Register as Visitor
Librarian

Librarians

  • Manage the book catalogue
  • Issue and take back books
  • Calculate late fines
  • See every overdue loan
Apply as Librarian
Admin

Administrators

  • Approve new librarians
  • Manage every account
  • Set the library rules
  • Watch reports and activity

How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Register

Open Register, choose Student, Librarian or Visitor, and fill in the details that type needs.

2

Log in

Use the single login page. BookNest opens your own dashboard automatically. Librarians log in after an admin approves them.

3

Use the library

Borrow and renew books, book a reading-room pass, or manage the collection, depending on your account.
